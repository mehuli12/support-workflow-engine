# A charge I do not recognise

Most unexpected charges have a simple explanation:

- **Subscription renewal:** your subscription renews automatically each month on the date you signed up.
- **Order sent in 2 parcels:** if your order ships in 2 parcels, we charge for each parcel when it leaves our warehouse. For example, a £50 order may show as £30 and £20 on different days.

To check a charge:

- Look at "My orders" and "My subscriptions" in your account.
- Compare the amount and date with your bank statement.
- If you were charged twice or the amount is wrong, we will refund the difference.

If you think the charge is fraud, contact us straight away.
