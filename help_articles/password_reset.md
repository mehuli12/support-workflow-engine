# Password Reset

Forgotten your password? You can reset it yourself in under 2 minutes.

- Click "Forgot password" on the login page.
- Enter the email address linked to your account.
- We will email you a reset link. It works for 30 minutes.
- If the email does not arrive, check your spam folder.

If the link has expired, request a new one. If you can no longer access your email, contact customer support.
